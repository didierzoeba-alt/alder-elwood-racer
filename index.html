How to play

1. Select Start race.
2. Use the arrow keys or A and D to steer.
3. Press W or the up arrow to accelerate. Hold SPACE for nitro.
4. On a phone, use the LEFT, BOOST and RIGHT buttons below the game.
5. Avoid traffic and rival cars. If you crash, select Race again.

About this game

An independent browser-based 3D racing project. It is inspired by the energy of arcade racers, but is not an official Asphalt game. This build adds a more detailed custom car, nitro management and a refined racing interface. Full AAA realism requires professionally modelled vehicle assets, advanced physics, audio and extensive optimization.
